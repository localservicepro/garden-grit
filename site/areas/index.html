Why a Norwood-based horticulturist is the local choice

The eastern suburbs share a few things: heavy clay under a thin layer of topsoil, big established trees, long formal hedges and gardens that were planted decades ago and need an experienced hand rather than a reset. Being based in Norwood means I am ten minutes from Burnside, Walkerville, Magill or St Peters, which keeps call-out time short and makes regular maintenance affordable. The north-eastern suburbs of Campbelltown, Modbury and Tea Tree Gully bring more new builds, new lawns and new gardens, and the inner west around Bowden and Prospect a mix of cottages and courtyards. All of it is within the 20 km radius.

Services across every suburb: landscape design and softscaping, garden maintenance and clean ups, hedge trimming, tree pruning, turf installation and irrigation.
